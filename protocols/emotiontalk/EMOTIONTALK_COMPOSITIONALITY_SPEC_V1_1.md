# Pairwise-to-Tri-modal Compositionality Spec V1.1

Frozen before tri-modal outputs. Construct pairwise directed edges only for source-label-conflict pairs with a valid side choice. Consistent pairs are SAME_LABEL and do not create a directional winner edge; OTHER/PARSE_FAIL is UNRESOLVED. Keep reciprocal orders paired. A stable edge requires both reciprocal orders to choose the same side; opposing choices are ORDER_SENSITIVE and remain order-specific.

Stable prediction is pattern-specific: TWO_VS_ONE requires its two cross-group conflict edges to be stable and agree on the shared-versus-minority winner; ALL_DIFFERENT requires all three conflict edges to be stable and transitive with a unique top label. For each exact tri-modal presentation, order-conditioned prediction uses only the pairwise outputs matching the relative order of each conflict pair. For TWO_VS_ONE, both cross-group conflict edges must agree; the shared/shared pair is SAME_LABEL. Disagreement, cycle, or unresolved edge gives UNRESOLVED. For ALL_DIFFERENT, require a unique Condorcet winner; cycle or unresolved edge gives UNRESOLVED. For TWO_VS_ONE, both conflict edges between shared and minority labels must agree; the shared/shared edge is SAME_LABEL.

Primary reporting: prediction coverage, resolved accuracy, overall eligible correct rate, cycle rate, unresolved rate. Six tri-modal orders remain T>A>V, T>V>A, A>T>V, A>V>T, V>T>A, V>A>T.
