# Known limitations

 EmotionTalk downstream eligibility depends on preceding model outputs by design. Raw data cannot supply newly inferred Readable/DualReadable flags. Frozen records support exact reuse; new inference can alter downstream eligibility and must not silently replace the mainline population.

 MCR uses the validated frozen derived artifact at the documented canonical-to-model-input boundary. Its old candidate producer is not reintroduced; corrected B3 uses the exact frozen target assignment.

 LISTEN source-probability transfer is structurally unsupported. The margin analysis is a sensitivity role, not a newly supported primary.

 CA-MER full primary source-probability metric is undefined outside its supported ontology role. The 416/672 coverage is conditional supported supplementary coverage; full margin coverage is 1350/2000.

 Cross-regime transfer is heterogeneous, not universal. Ranking performance and balanced accuracy at the source threshold differ. MCR BA is 0.5 despite AUROC about 0.728. LISTEN's descriptive AUROC crossing does not establish strong transfer or statistical significance.

 The original Phase3 primary-plan and complete main-analysis writers were not recovered. The frozen plan, source-model composition rule, summary tables and limited inferential audit are preserved evidence; this release does not claim a new complete inferential reimplementation. The report command now independently joins recovered observed Phase2/Phase3 responses and derives 268 correct identities among 294 resolved predictions. This covers only the 67 TWO_VS_ONE utterances; the three ALL_DIFFERENT eligible utterances are outside the frozen primary population. Minimal source-standardized CV records and all required Phase2 responses are included with derivation/projection hashes. Historical CV diagnostics inside immutable source-binding evidence are not current corrected publication results.

 The frozen GPU environment includes a transformers development version; obtain that exact archived build/environment. CPU tests use a separate recorded environment. GPU numerical reruns were not performed during packaging. New portable launchers reuse confirmed scientific kernels and write independent output paths; their orchestration validation is CPU/static, not a claim of a second validated GPU execution.

 Necessary historical scientific result evidence is now tracked in this private repository. No public archival URL is assigned or invented. Authorized repository access permits independent CPU metric replay; public access and complete raw-media/inference reproducibility are separate access-dependent boundaries.


LISTEN pooled 518 rows mix 3A/3B/3C and cannot serve as joint arbitration performance. The joint-only 3C analysis evaluates 167/378 conflicts and uses A_THEN_T exclusively, preventing within-3C counterbalanced order-effect identification. Its transferred-model log loss is worse than source-fixed M0 despite AUROC above 0.5. MCR is later-added/exploratory relative to the early transfer declaration, and its very imbalanced outcomes explain why useful probability/ranking metrics coexist with BA 0.5. Dataset AUROC rankings do not establish a common mechanism ranking.

Frozen source M3a/M3b use pair-specific intercepts with shared strength, entropy and presentation-order slopes; they are pooled fits rather than AT-specific models or pair-specific order-effect estimates. Post-audit M0+order is source-only diagnostic evidence, not an originally declared frozen model. Cluster percentile intervals are exploratory post-audit intervals, not an executable acceptance rule or a complete multiplicity-adjusted inferential reconstruction.

MCR has three byte-identical content groups affecting seven frozen samples; one group has conflicting GT. Historical 909-sample selection and metrics remain unchanged. The separate exclusion sensitivity uses all seven affected IDs, not a target-performance-selected subset. Raw WAV access is necessary to independently repeat audio hashing; the supplied actual-hash trace differs from merely stored canonical hashes. Restricted datasets/media and model weights are not included or licensed by MIT.
